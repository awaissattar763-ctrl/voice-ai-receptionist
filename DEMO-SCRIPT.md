# Demo Video Script — AI Voice Receptionist for Clinics (60 seconds)

**Setup:** open the public demo page (`/`), start screen recording, keep the
phone simulator centered at ~1280px browser width. Speak naturally — timings
are approximate.

---

**[0:00–0:08] Hook**

> "What happens when a patient calls your clinic at 9:47 PM? Voicemail. And by
> morning, they've booked with someone else. This is Ava — an AI voice
> receptionist that picks up every call."

*Action: phone simulator visible on the 'After-hours emergency' scenario card.*

**[0:08–0:30] Booking path**

> "First, a normal daytime call. Watch the transcript play out like a real
> phone call."

*Action: tap the 'Appointment booking' scenario → tap 'Start demo call'. Let
the transcript play — point at the AI label and the caller label as they
alternate.*

> "Greeting, slot offer, name and number captured, booking confirmed with an
> SMS on the way — all by voice, no staff involved."

*Action: let it finish; point at the green 'Start demo call' → the call
summary card ('Appointment booked').*

**[0:30–0:48] Emergency escalation**

> "Now the call that actually loses you patients — after hours."

*Action: tap the 'After-hours emergency' scenario → 'Start demo call'.*

> "Severe pain and swelling — Ava detects the emergency, pages the on-call
> dentist, and holds the first urgent slot for tomorrow morning. A real
> emergency never hits voicemail."

*Action: point at the amber 'Emergency detected' system badge mid-call, then
the summary card with the follow-up flag.*

**[0:48–0:56] Dashboard**

> "And everything lands here — every call, every transcript, every outcome,
> with follow-ups flagged."

*Action: switch to `/admin`, tap 'Load sample data', expand one transcript.*

**[0:56–1:00] Close**

> "This is a concept demo — but the same flow wires up to Twilio and
> ElevenLabs on your real phone line. If missed calls are costing you
> patients, let's talk."

---

**Recording tips**

- Run each scenario once off-camera to warm up, then 'Clear demo data' on the
  admin page right before recording for a clean take.
- Don't rush — the AI 'typing…' indicator and waveform are the realism; let
  each line land.
- Keep the waveform visible while the AI 'speaks' — it's the visual proof of
  voice.
- End on the admin dashboard with the emergency call's transcript expanded.
